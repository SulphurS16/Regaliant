# 5. What remains open at 0ee206e

## A bright tunnel, bad geometry and black textures

The fork's tunnel exposure regression has a reproducible switch. The checker holds the camera still and compares the game's real display buffer at guest flips 3600, 3900 and 4200, before swapchain presentation, with frame generation off. The upstream fast-clear ownership handoff allows a new render target to claim compression metadata previously associated with another. With colour handoff enabled, mean RGB in the saved tunnel was about 0.60 on a 0–1 encoded scale. Disabling it gave about 0.07. Depth-only handoff worked in this comparison. Linux now claims depth metadata only by default (`e4d2ae6`). The comparison excludes generated images and points away from uffd as the cause. It narrows the fault to the colour handoff without explaining exactly which target received which wrong clear. The diagnostic guide preserves earlier candidate defaults and experiments. [Exposure checks](../linux-exposure-check.md)

Oversized glows, stray geometry and some black textures remain in the fork README. A skipped pipeline can explain missing draws after interrupted compiles (`0084b1d`), and a wrongly retained attachment after resizing was fixed in `54c4965`. Neither observation identifies every dark surface. The play diagnostic compares the same camera with generation off/on, depth-clear changes and texture detiling controls. Counts of zero-filled subresources can include multiple reports for one texture. Likewise, the cube-face translation fix explains the driver abort for four observed variants but leaves open why gameplay learned the suspect cube descriptors alongside an unusually large allocation request. [Play diagnostics](../linux-play-diagnostics.md) · [Cube-fetch evidence](../linux-cube-fetch.md)

The final Palace replay tested whether a missing image-view LOD clamp explained zero-filled texture mips. With the new T# clamp enabled, the runtime created no nonzero-clamp view. All 1,536 freshly translated shaders lacked both the explicit clamp form examined and TFE/LWE residency requests. That eliminates those ignored inputs as the explanation for that replay. The change supplies the descriptor behavior where the device supports it, but shaders requesting residency status elsewhere still lack a translated status result. Which producer failed to populate each black texture remains open (`0ee206e`). [Linux texture investigation](04-linux.md)

## Experiments with unresolved correctness costs

Whole-run parallel recording (`DS_PAR_WHOLE_RUN=1`) hands a synchronization-free stretch to a worker and records primary batches concurrently in submission order. Boundary screenshots and packet coverage looked plausible. Moving gameplay showed green shapes, flicker and black pops. The state or resource handoff is unresolved. Pending clears, label-only batches, mirror visibility and speculative end-state validation remain candidates. It stays off and is incompatible with frame generation. [Linux maintainer notes](../linux-notes.md)

Frame generation can repair invalid previous motion for SDK consumption, but the originating draw remains unidentified. Camera-based motion only approximates a moving object. A generated still can look plausible while movement shows HUD smear, halos or newly exposed surfaces filled incorrectly. Legacy pacing adds GPU work and buffering. Adaptive pacing has a measured application-side result but lacks physical latency validation. Temporary snapshot pressure can skip generation and retry. Uses with no known completion sequence can remain pinned. A long resize session may need a restart. [Interpolation limits](../linux-frame-generation.md)

## Throughput, stalls and long sessions

Busy areas remain slow on Linux. The Palace comparisons isolate wait notification, scanning and remap costs under particular loads. They cannot be added together to predict a general frame rate. Cold compiles, screenshot readback, validation and thermal pauses can each dominate a short sample. The native-4K steady procedure warms the route, measures 30 seconds stationary and 60 walking, and moves wait diagnosis to a separate session (`e35c77d`, `1b2a9dd`). The Nexus procedure asks for actual binary hits. Simply reaching a menu can conceal a refused binary or a quarantined draw. Preparation can only cover variants encountered on its routes. [Nexus checks](../linux-nexus-check.md) · [Linux performance procedure](../linux.md)

The uffd tour completed. The repaired mprotect fallback has race tests but lacks comparable tour coverage. Optional page arming reduced checks at the Palace. Extending that result to C4 would require safely delaying GPU-to-CPU visibility, which arming alone cannot establish. Linux keeps eager write-back (`d41b72b`). Audio buffering changes and movie clock corrections address specific issues, while the fork still reports audio dropouts in long sessions. The sources do not establish a single remaining cause. [Fork status](../../README.md) · [Memory constraints](../linux-notes.md)

## Limits of the testing record

The upstream Windows figure of about 40–55 fps in the busiest areas belongs to the Ryzen 9 5900X / RTX 4090 test PC. The Linux fork reports one NVIDIA test PC. Reaching areas and bosses with developer shortcuts leaves progression, saves and transitions through a complete uninterrupted playthrough unverified. Online messages, bloodstains, invasions and co-op are unavailable. Upstream also records water banding, a darker opening sky, equipment-preview and character-creation defects, unverified HDR display output and limited hardware coverage. A simulated 16 GB configuration cannot establish behavior on a real 16 GB PC. Low VRAM can force images into system memory, increasing frame time. [Upstream known issues](../known-issues.md) · [Hardware scope](../requirements.md)

The upstream issue list's Linux support statement describes the Windows release. The experimental fork can run gameplay, resume saves and complete its shader tour. Its graphical launcher, HDR, exclusive display-mode switching, compositor VRR control and DLSS/XeSS/FSR upscaling remain outside the implemented Linux feature set. The optional FSR3 interpolation path is separate. Controller output and movie audio need broader physical-device testing. [Fork status](../../README.md) · [Linux limitations](../linux.md)
